What this can and cannot do from the browser

You can: download prices and rates, import your own price files, run any strategy in the strategies folder, read and compare results, re-run with different settings, search papers and drop PDFs into the library.

Writing a new strategy from a description or a paper needs Claude Code (it writes the strategy file). New strategy files appear here automatically once they are in the strategies/ folder.
