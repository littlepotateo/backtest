# Is Trend Still Your Friend?: A Microstructural Account of the Demise of Short-Term Trend-Following

Jutta G. Kurth et al. (2026). arXiv

https://consensus.app/papers/details/w:169eb69a-256b-4736-8de0-33899d725512/?utm_source=claude_desktop

## Key finding

Using about 100 futures contracts over 1995-2025, documents that short-term trends stopped delivering reliable returns after about 2009 and links the break to volatility-normalised tick size.
